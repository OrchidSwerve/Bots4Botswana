Get Involved

There's a role here for you

Whether it's a kit sitting in your closet, an hour of your time, or a company willing to sponsor a shipment — every contribution moves a kit closer to a classroom.

Monetary Donations

Fund a shipment

Financial donations cover the shipping, customs, replacement parts, and educational resources that turn a collected kit into a working classroom tool. Every dollar is allocated against a specific need — see the full breakdown on our Strategy & Sustainability page.

Do you have a kit to donate?

We accept LEGO Mindstorms EV3 and NXT kits and expansion sets in any condition — even incomplete sets can often be refurbished using parts from others.

Arrange a Kit Donation
Robotics Kit Donations

Give a retired kit a second classroom

If your robotics team, school, or family has a Mindstorms kit that's no longer in use, it can be refurbished and put back to work in a classroom in Botswana. We handle pickup or shipping coordination and inspect every kit before it's cleared for donation.

Corporate Sponsorships

Sponsor a shipment or a program

Companies can sponsor a full kit shipment, provide in-kind support like shipping discounts or refurbishment supplies, or back a specific part of our program, like teacher training. Sponsors are recognized on our Partners page.

Discuss Sponsorship

What sponsorship supports

  • A full shipment of refurbished kits
  • Replacement parts and sensors
  • Teacher training materials
  • Expansion into a new partner school
