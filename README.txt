Drop your photos in this folder using these exact filenames and each will
appear automatically on the site (no HTML edits needed):

about.html
  founder-headshot.jpg      - circular headshot
  kit-refurbishment.jpg     - photo of a kit being refurbished/tested

index.html
  kits-collected.jpg        - photo of collected kits
  students-building.jpg     - photo of students building/programming

impact.html
  partner-school.jpg        - photo of a partner school/classroom
  students-programming.jpg  - photo of students programming a kit
  robot-build-closeup.jpg   - close-up of an assembled robot

get-involved.html
  kit-donation-boxes.jpg    - photo of kits packed for donation

tutorials.html
  tutorial-in-use.jpg       - photo of a tutorial/guide in use

Any image format works (jpg, png, webp) as long as the filename
(including extension) matches exactly. Until a file is added, that
spot shows a placeholder box telling you which file it's waiting for.
